Questions? Answers.

How does the waiting period work?

Add any item you're tempted to buy and choose a delay from 1 to 30 days. While it's locked, you can't buy on impulse — when the timer ends, you decide calmly: purchase, pass, or wait longer.

Is Impulses free?

Yes. Impulses is free to download. Premium unlocks custom delays, unlimited items, and deeper stats — subscriptions are optional.

Where is my data stored?

On your device by default. Optional iCloud sync keeps items across your Apple devices in your private database — we don't host or sell your purchase list.

What resets my streak?

Rejecting an impulse keeps your streak alive. Accepting a purchase resets it — a simple way to measure consistency, not perfection.
